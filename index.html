Why this trip matters

The DHDE model already tracks Fukui's own sites with edge-AI camera counts, JMA weather, search-intent and hotel data. Fieldwork days are the benchmark and ground-truth side of that model — including some of Fukui's own sites, plus mature high-traffic comparisons in Osaka, Kyoto and Kanazawa. Everything logged here feeds the suppressed-demand and congestion forecasts.

Route for this day

Field conduct

  • No fixed roles — split up by category naturally when you arrive, and rotate if it's quiet.
  • Buddy up — never survey a service provider or intercept a visitor alone.
  • Ask permission before quoting a business owner, recording a voice note, or photographing an identifiable face.
  • Charge phones at breakfast; pack a portable battery. Open this app once over wifi before you leave — it then works offline all day.
  • Halal note: on days that flag it, meals may not be reliable on-site — pack accordingly.

How your data reaches everyone

This app has no server — everything (entries, photos, voice notes) stays on your phone until you export it. At each break, open Log, tap Export, and send the file to whoever is compiling. They paste it into Merge in a teammate's export on their own phone. The compiler can also commit the merged file into the team's GitHub repo so it's never only on one phone — see the README.
